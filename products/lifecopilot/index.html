AI mobile app

Life Copilota companion for living the city better

An AI app for organizing routines, understanding personal taste and suggesting plans, restaurants, travel ideas and activities based on the context of your city.

Target user

Urban people

Designed for everyday people who want to organize life, discover what to do and make better personal decisions without jumping between disconnected apps.

Contextual intelligence

Learns personal taste

Life Copilot can learn preferences such as Japanese food, coffee shops, Asian culture or travel, then recommend relevant options in the city where the user is located.

Routines and plans

Always something meaningful to do

The app helps plan routines, outings, purchases, experiences, cultural activities and trips by connecting personal habits with real local opportunities.

Local experiences

Plans near you

When someone wants coffee, a new restaurant or an interesting activity, Life Copilot can recommend options based on location, preferences and current context.

Launch

iOS and Android

The product does not yet have an active domain or live app. It is planned as a mobile application for iOS and Android devices.

Main benefit

A more organized and interesting life

The goal is to give each person an intelligent companion to discover, plan, learn and enjoy what is happening around them.
