Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to start a commercial conversation with realistic expectations about scope, timing, security and support.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A diagnosis may take a few days; a functional prototype usually takes weeks; a platform or full implementation is defined in phases to reduce risk.

How is cost defined?

It is estimated based on scope, complexity, integrations, support level, urgency and deliverables. That is why the recommended first step is a diagnosis conversation.

Do you work with confidential information?

Yes. We can work under confidentiality agreements and limit access to sensitive data according to project needs and company policies.

What happens with intellectual property?

Intellectual property is agreed by contract depending on the type of project. We can build custom software for the client or develop reusable components under clear conditions.

Does the company need an internal technical team?

Not necessarily. Tecnotitan can support from diagnosis to deployment. If an internal team already exists, we work as a technical partner to accelerate architecture, development and adoption.

Do you offer support after deployment?

Yes. We can include maintenance, evolutionary improvements, monitoring, documentation, training and operational support depending on the required service level.

How do you handle security and data?

We design each solution considering permissions, access control, traceability, minimal data exposure, suitable providers and responsible deployment practices.

Can we start with something small?

Yes. We recommend starting with a diagnosis, MVP or measurable pilot. That helps validate value before committing larger investments.
